Research Statement & Publication Status

Research Statement

My path into robotics research began in industrial substations and on a family poultry farm, not in a classroom — two years keeping generators running and a small business alive through the pandemic taught me that engineering is, first, about delivering reliably under constraint. That instinct now anchors my research in autonomous navigation.

I am currently developing a fully autonomous mobile robot capable of real-time mapping and navigation on low-cost, resource-constrained edge hardware, applied to autonomous disinfection in hospital and clinical environments. My work spans the full stack: hardware integration (LiDAR, Kinect RGB-D, Arduino Mega, NVIDIA Jetson Nano) and software architecture (ROS2, SLAM Toolbox, Nav2).

I am actively seeking fully funded MSc or Research Assistant positions in autonomous systems, embedded AI, and robotics for 2027–28. My research interests include SLAM, multi-sensor fusion, edge AI for autonomous systems, and AI-integrated automation pipelines.
