~/projects/ultron-uv in progress

Ultron UV

Ultron UV is a research prototype of an autonomous disinfection robot, built by Team Supersonic UAP, a three-person team I lead. It maps and navigates indoor spaces using LiDAR and Kinect RGB-D on a Jetson Nano with an Arduino Mega motor layer, running ROS2, SLAM Toolbox and Nav2, with remote monitoring over Zenoh and Tailscale. Featured at the BEAR Innovation Expo 2026 in Dhaka.

Status: on runnable trials. What works today: Autonomously navigate only in suitable environments (non-crowded places). My role: architecture, integration, hardware.

Autonomous Mobile Robot Navigation via ROS2-Based SLAM and AI-Enhanced Decision-Making

Sadnan Sajid et al. · University of Asia Pacific, Bangladesh

Research on autonomous mobile robot navigation integrating ROS2-based SLAM with AI-enhanced decision-making. The system addresses real-time mapping and navigation challenges using SLAM Toolbox, Nav2, and sensor fusion with LiDAR and RGB-D cameras on resource-constrained edge hardware (NVIDIA Jetson Nano). Evaluation focuses on map consistency under sensor noise and navigation performance in dynamic environments.
